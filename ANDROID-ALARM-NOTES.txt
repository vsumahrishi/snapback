For stronger closed-app alarms on the final APK:
1. Phone: Apps → SnapBack → Notifications → Unrestricted
2. Phone: Apps → SnapBack → Battery → Unrestricted
3. Some OEMs: Alarms & reminders → allow SnapBack
4. After installing APK: Settings in app → Grant permission → Save Bug Me again
5. Optional for developers: SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM in AndroidManifest (Capacitor android module)
