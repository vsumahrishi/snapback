SnapBack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

SnapBack is a personal productivity and study-support application. This policy describes what information is handled when you use the SnapBack website or mobile app.

Information you provide

Depending on how you sign in, SnapBack may store information you enter such as your name, email address, phone number (if you choose to provide it), tasks, journal entries, schedule items, and app settings.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic account information such as your name and email address to SnapBack for authentication. SnapBack uses this to identify your account and sync your app data across your devices when cloud sync is enabled.

How information is used

Your information is used to operate the app, save your preferences, provide reminders, and sync your data when you are signed in. Contact details you choose to provide may be stored so the app operator can understand who is using the app and communicate important updates.

Storage

Data may be stored on your device and, when cloud features are enabled, in Firebase services associated with the SnapBack project. Local-only accounts keep data on the device unless you enable cloud sync.

Sharing

SnapBack does not sell your personal information. Calendar share links only show the availability information you choose to share. Service providers such as Google Firebase may process data to provide authentication and storage.

Contact

For privacy questions about SnapBack, contact the app operator using the developer contact email listed on the OAuth consent screen or project settings.
